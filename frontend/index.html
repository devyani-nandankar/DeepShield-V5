Explainable Multimodal Verification Pipeline

How DeepShield decomposes digital media into verifiable spatial, temporal, and attribution streams.

1

Face Localization

OpenCV YuNet neural face detector isolates the primary facial geometry using a standardized square 1:1 crop with margin retention.

2

Deep Spatial Analysis

EfficientNetB2 extracts 1,408-dimensional spatial representations trained to detect boundary blending, warping, and GAN synthesis artifacts.

3

Temporal Coherence

For video streams, DeepShield V5.2 samples keyframes and feeds sequential representations through BiLSTM networks to audit inter-frame consistency.

4

Explainable Attribution

Grad-CAM computes gradients at top convolution layers, generating JET saliency heatmaps that highlight exact pixel clusters driving the verdict.
